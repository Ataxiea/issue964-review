Druid-corpus proc observations for #964 — review draft (2026-11-20, rev 2)

Not posted upstream. 505 druid-containing public reports (forever.warcraftlogs.com, zone 3000), every page SHA-256-verified; 6,368 fights; 39.5M events. Of the issue's 431 catalog rows this pass measures 43 (all listed in measured_rows.csv); nine of the 160 still-open (⏳) rows carry observations below. These are observed counts, not "measured" rows — the issue's own bar (EV-0136) is 5+ wearers with a 95% interval within ±10%, which none of the nine meet here.

Method

Per-wearer gear from fight-scoped combatant_info only. A proc is any event of the item's payload spell, counted once when one proc logs several events within 50 ms (EV-0153's event policy), and every onset is attributed to the wearer's form at its first event. The rate is clustered onsets ÷ (landed eligible hits × swing speed ÷ 60), with numerator and denominator restricted to the same fights — fights whose hits are all pre-snapshot censored, or whose procs exceed their hits, are excluded and counted, never silently dropped. Intervals: exact Poisson 95% on proc counts, and the wider EV-0136 whole-wearer bootstrap (2,000 draws) where 2+ wearers exist. Denominator caveat: eligible hits are white swings of the item's hand (plus the paw for forms). Hits the proc flags hear beyond white — specials, seals, Judgements, Windfury strikes (EV-0155) — are not counted, so rows worn by special-heavy classes read high by that factor; druid form strata have the complete denominator (form specials roll no weapon proc, EV-0022). The isolated-window count (procs matched to a single candidate hit within 80 ms) is a censoring-biased lower bound, carried in the CSVs, never published as a rate.

Left out, and why

Stoneslayer and Flame Wrath-style onsets include secondary fire paths (Flame Wrath's clustered form rates still land at EV-0151's corrected ~2.2–2.4, so its form rows are shown above with this note); per-cast payloads (Kindling Stave, Verigan's Fist, Warblade of Caer Darrow, Wolfsbane, Heartseeking Crossbow, Hurricane — 30–300 by the row convention) are excluded; dual-wield rows stay unresolved on hand attribution. Strike of the Hydra: upstream's 2.12–2.23 stands; this corpus's 3.99 reads high by the same white-only factor.

Wearer-class and screen flags

EV-0153's owner ruling excludes paladin wearers from every rate: Angerforge's Battle Axe and Witchfury are worn by paladins here (identified by the review council from talent trees) and are shown for completeness only. Heart of Wyrmthalak's bear stratum (0.40 payloads/hit) and the Crusader / Fiery Weapon receipt rows (89–92% of their counts from fights with no counted denominator) are quarantined by the payloads-per-hit screen. Hasted cats (~15% of cat fights) swing faster than the 1.0 s base; base-speed pricing keeps the PPM model unbiased but actual procs-per-minute for those wearers reads ~15% higher than the published base-speed figure.

Raw data and reproducibility

Full receipt (r20) · Channel census (153 spells) · Inventory ledger · Normalization verdicts (onset-based) · CSVs: open_rows, form_strata, per_report, measured_rows. The receipts regenerate from the page-verified ledger with one command on the machine holding the tier-2 page store; externally, re-fetch the named reports from Warcraft Logs and hash-compare against the receipt's per-report inventory. Multiple-comparison note: the intervals above are per-comparison 95%; across 26+ simultaneous rows the family-wise error is higher — treat tight CIs on few-wearer rows accordingly.
